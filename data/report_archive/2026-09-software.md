# Lather Log - September 2026

**Total Shaves:** 1,298
**Unique Shavers:** 99

Welcome to your SOTD Lather Log for September 2026

* 1,298 shave reports from 99 distinct shavers during the month of September 2026 were analyzed to produce this report. Collectively, these shavers used 526 distinct soaps from 126 distinct brands. 30 of those shaves used samples or smushes of 17 unique soaps, and 5 used mashups.

## Observations

* Stirling Soap Co. eases to the top of the Brands table for the fifth straight month, but the real fight was one step down with Barrister and Mann holding off Catie's Bubbles by a single shave, 100 to 99.

* Catie's Bubbles – Tonsorium took the top step of the Soaps table for its first time ever. u/Glass_Procedure7497 did most of the work, posting 29 of its 35 shaves.

* Silver was a one-shaver production too: u/tsrblke logged all 22 of Barrister and Mann – Oceana's shaves, the soap's first podium finish since April 2021.

* Seaforth! Épée makes its Lather Log debut at #14 and hauls the whole Spearhead Shaving Company banner up 18 spots to #7.

* Brand Diversity reverted to standard programming: Stirling's 48 distinct soaps put it 17 clear of silver medalist Barrister and Mann (31), with Catie's Bubbles on the bronze step at 29. .

* u/sandstorm99 didn't get the message the Austere Augest ended at the end of August. He stuck to one soap, one razor, one blade, one brush for all 19 of his September shaves, earning him the coveted Most Boring Shaver crown this month.

* u/partyman97_3 wins the Soap Diversity by User trophy with 30 unique soaps in 31 shaves, one repeat away from perfection. u/snoo-ting actually went perfect at 27-for-27, but the contest rewards max distinct count, not perfect ratios.

* u/Engineered_Shave nabbed the Exclusive Soaps title with 24 soaps that only he used.

* Sample September 2026 fell far short of 2025. 30 sample shaves from 7 users (17 different soaps) was half of last September's 60 from 19 users.

## Notes & Caveats

* Tables show all entries with 5 or more shaves to provide comprehensive coverage without artificial row limits.

* The unique user column shows the number of different users who used a given brand/soap/etc in the month.

* The Brand Diversity table details the number of distinct soaps used during the month from that particular brand, showing all brands with 5+ unique soaps.

* The change Δ vs columns show how an item has moved up or down the rankings since that month. = means no change in position, up or down arrows indicate how many positions up or down the rankings an item has moved compared to that month. n/a means the item was not present in that month.

* When available, soap names in the Soaps table link to their entry on Wet Shaving Database, where you can see scent notes, descriptions, and ingredient lists.

* An **exclusive soap** is a matched brand+scent used by only one shaver in the month. That shaver earns one point for that soap, no matter how many times they logged it.

## Most Boring Shaver
Boring Score is the chance that two shaves picked at random from a user used the same soap (higher means more repetitive). Effective Soaps converts that into an equivalent even-rotation count: the number of soaps they would need to use equally often to produce the same score. Example: someone can log 20 soaps but still have ~2 Effective Soaps if one soap dominates roughly 70% of their shaves and the rest are just occasional. In the case of tied HHI, Shaves descending is the tie breaker, since it's clearly more boring to use the same soap for 20 shaves than it is for 10.

For the mathematically inclined: Boring Score is a diversity/concentration index (HHI in economics, Simpson’s index in ecology). More detail at [Wikipedia: Diversity index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_index).
